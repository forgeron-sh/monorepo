---
title: "The draw editor renders on the GPU at every zoom"
summary: "Every kind of item, words included, is now drawn on the GPU, near and far, and panning no longer restyles the board."
date: 2026-11-20
type: Improved
areas: [Editors]
---

- Every kind of item is drawn on the GPU at every zoom, not only when zoomed out.
- Words are drawn on the GPU too, from a glyph atlas.
- Items far from the camera are drawn on the GPU by `@forgeron/canvas`.
- Panning no longer restyles and re-renders the editors on every frame.
