- `@forgeron/richtext` is the page editor of the platform, on Tiptap (ProseMirror), with Yjs wired in for collaboration.
- Every feature is a plugin on a minimal core. A plugin has two halves: the core one (the document and its Markdown) and the editor one (its controls).
- The document is JSON, with a block catalog, Markdown in and out, and a static renderer that needs no editor.
- `richtext.forgeron.sh` lists every plugin with a live demo, a playground and a guide.
