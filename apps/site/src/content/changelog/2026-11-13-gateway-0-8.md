### New in 0.8.0

- Each call to a tool reports the time of every step in the `Server-Timing` header (D-142).

### Improved in 0.8.1 to 0.8.4

- A call reads the registry once and remembers it (D-143).
- A cold call reads the tool, its access and its teams in fewer trips.
- The directory reads its members, teams and grants together.
- A call is prepared while its tool is looked up.
- A call's reads go to D1 in one batch.
