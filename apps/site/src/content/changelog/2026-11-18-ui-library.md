- Field primitives: combobox, slider, time, money, split pane and more.
- Blocks by kind of tool: admin, approval and signature, automation, conversation, dashboard, data hub, drive, finance, forms, structure, time, editor chrome and marketing.
- An icon picker block, searchable, that loads the catalog lazily.
- `ui.forgeron.sh` shows every component with its examples, its code and its props.
