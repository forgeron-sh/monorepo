- `@forgeron/flow` (diagrams) and `@forgeron/draw` (a whiteboard) run on `@forgeron/canvas`, the engine of the editors.
- In both, every feature is a plugin on a minimal core.
- The engine answers its queries from a spatial index.
- Realistic demo boards for both editors ship in `@forgeron/libraries`.
